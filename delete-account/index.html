Delete your account

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page is for players of Two Minute Drill, made by Good Luck Machine, LLC. Here’s how to delete your account, what goes, what stays, and when.

In the game (fastest)

Open Settings > Help & Privacy > Delete Account and confirm. You’ll carry on as a new guest right away. If you sign in with a username, Game Center or Google Play Games, you have 14 days to change your mind: sign in to the account again and choose Keep my account. After that, it’s gone for good. If you play as a guest, there’s nothing to sign back in with, so the deletion can’t be undone.

Without the game

Can’t open the game, or lost your phone? Fill in the deletion request form, or email support@goodluckmachine.com. Your Player ID helps us find the account (in the game, under Settings > Help & Privacy). Without one, tell us the display name shown in the game and how you signed in.

How we check it’s your account

Anyone can type a Player ID into a form, so a person reads every request from the website before anything is deleted. We’ll reply within 3 business days and ask you a couple of things only the account’s owner would know, such as:

  • your display name, and roughly when you started playing
  • your team’s name and colours
  • how you sign in, and your Game Center or Google Play Games nickname or username
  • a store receipt, if you ever bought anything

We never ask for ID documents, photos, or anything we don’t already hold. Once the answers match, we set the account for deletion and email you the date.

If we can’t confirm the account is yours, we don’t delete it, and we’ll tell you why. That can happen with guest accounts: a guest account has no email or sign-in tied to it, so if the game is gone from your phone and you can’t remember its details, nothing we hold can show it was yours. The in-game option above always works while the game is installed.

How long it takes

We delete your account within 30 days of your request. The first 14 days are yours to change your mind. For a request from the website, the 30 days start once we’ve confirmed the account is yours.

What we delete

  • Your player, team and progress, and saved games.
  • Your League history and leaderboard entries.
  • Your name, and your username, email and password if you made them.
  • Your sign-in links to Game Center or Google Play Games on our side.
  • Game activity and session records tied to your player.

Deletion is permanent. Purchased items go with the account, and we can’t bring them back.

What stays, and for how long

DataWhat happens
Crash reportsKept up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
Install and attribution recordsHeld by our measurement provider, Singular. Ask us and we’ll send Singular an erasure request, which they complete within 30 days. Combined statistics that can’t identify you stay.
Gameplay analyticsKept for the times in How long we keep it, then deleted or combined so they can’t identify you.
Push notification tokenRemoved from your phone when you delete. If you allowed notifications, it may linger with Apple or Google until it expires.

What we can’t delete

Your Game Center profile belongs to Apple, and your Google Play Games profile belongs to Google. Deleting your account here doesn’t touch them. To remove the game from your Game Center profile, use the Game Center settings on your Apple device. For Google Play Games, use the Play Games app’s settings.

More on how we handle your data is in the Privacy Policy.
