What we collect

What you give us

  • Account info: an account ID, your display name, your email address, and how you signed in (an email login, or a platform sign-in like Apple or Google).
  • Messages to us: anything you send our way, plus any details you include.

What we pick up while you play

  • Gameplay: your progress, items and currency, settings, achievements, and how you use the Games’ features.
  • Your device: the model, operating system and version, language, app version, time zone, and IP address (we use it to work out roughly where you are, like your country). We also get crash and performance information.
  • Device and advertising IDs: an app-install ID, a device ID, and, if your settings allow it, your device’s advertising ID (Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID). On iOS, we only read the IDFA if you say yes to Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We use these IDs to run the Games, spot fraud, and find out which ads led to an install. There’s more in Ads and how we measure them.
  • Purchases: what you bought, when, and the transaction ID. Apple or Google handles the payment, so we never see your card details.
  • Website visits: standard server logs, like your IP address and browser type. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we don’t collect: your precise location, contacts, photos, or anything from your microphone or camera.

Ads and how we measure them

We advertise the Games on platforms like Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok. To find out which ads actually lead people to install and play, we work with Singular, a mobile measurement partner.

Here’s how it works. When you install and open a Game, the Singular SDK collects some device and technical data: your IP address, device model, OS version, app-install ID, and, if you allow it, your advertising ID. Singular matches that against ad clicks and views to figure out which campaign, if any, brought you to us. After that, we and Singular send a small set of in-game events (like install, tutorial complete, and purchase) back to the ad platform that ran the campaign, so it can measure and improve how our ads perform.

We don’t show other companies’ ads in the Games, and we don’t sell your data for money. But some US state laws count sending IDs and events to ad platforms like this as “sharing” for “cross-context behavioral advertising” or “targeted advertising.” You can opt out whenever you like. See below, and Your US state privacy rights.

Your controls

  • iOS: We ask before reading your IDFA, using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, we use Apple’s privacy-friendly SKAdNetwork / AdAttributionKit for attribution instead, and no advertising ID is used. You can change your mind any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: You can delete or reset your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (on some devices, Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • In the Games: Switch off Settings > Privacy > Share data for ad measurement. That stops us and Singular from sending your events to ad platforms, on every device where you’re signed in.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before reading your advertising ID or turning on attribution. If you say no, we collect no attribution data about you. You can withdraw consent any time in Settings > Privacy.

Your choices and rights

It’s your data, and you’re in charge of it. Depending on where you live, you may be able to see what we have, correct it, delete it, or get a copy; object to or limit how we use it; take back consent you gave; and complain to your data protection authority. We’ll never treat you differently for using any of these rights.

  • In the US: head to Your US state privacy rights.
  • In the EEA, UK, or Switzerland: our legal bases are listed above, and you can object to anything we do based on legitimate interests.
  • On your device: you can reset or limit your advertising ID and manage tracking permissions in your device settings.

How to ask. Email support@goodluckmachine.com from the address linked to your account, or include your player ID (you can find it in Settings > Account). That’s how we make sure it’s really you. We might ask for one more detail only the account holder would know, like a recent purchase. If you play without an email-linked account, we can verify you with a code shown in the game. We’ll never ask for ID documents.

How fast we reply. Within one month if you’re in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, and within 45 days in the US. If your request is complicated, the law lets us take a bit longer. If that happens, we’ll let you know.

If you’re not happy. If you’re in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

If we say no. You can appeal by replying to our decision with “Appeal” in the subject line. We’ll answer within the time your state’s law requires. If you’re still not happy, you can contact your state attorney general.

Deleting your account

Want to leave? You can ask us to delete your account and the data that goes with it at any time. Do it in the Games (Settings), where that’s available, or email support@goodluckmachine.com from the address linked to your account with the subject “Account deletion request.” Heads up: deletion is permanent. Your progress and purchased in-game items go with it, and we can’t bring them back.

The full steps, including a web form if you can’t open the Game, are on our Delete your account page.

Kids and age limits

Our Games aren’t made for children, and you need to be at least 13 to play. That’s the minimum in the UK too. In the EEA, it’s the age your country sets for agreeing to online services, somewhere between 13 and 16.

The first time you open a Game, we ask your age, without hinting at the “right” answer. If you’re under the minimum age, you can’t create an account, and we don’t collect anything about you beyond what we need to keep you out. If you’re under 18, we turn off sharing for ad measurement and use privacy-friendly defaults.

If we find out we’ve collected personal data from a child under the minimum age, we’ll delete it. And if you think a child has given us their information, please tell us at support@goodluckmachine.com.
