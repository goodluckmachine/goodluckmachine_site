Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data: an account identifier, display name, email address, and sign-in method (for example, an email login or a platform sign-in such as Apple or Google).
  • Support and communications: messages you send us and any details you include.

Data we collect automatically

  • Gameplay data: progress, in-game items and currency, settings, achievements, and how you interact with features.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, IP address (from which we infer approximate location such as country), time zone, and crash and performance information.
  • Device and advertising identifiers. These include an app-install ID, a device ID, and, where your settings allow, your device’s advertising identifier: Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID. On iOS we read the IDFA only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We use these identifiers to run the Games, prevent fraud, and measure which ads led to an install. See Advertising measurement and attribution below.

  • Website data: standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone or camera data.

Advertising measurement and attribution

We advertise the Games on platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok. To learn which ads lead people to install and play, we use Singular, a mobile measurement partner.

When you install and open a Game, the Singular SDK collects device and technical data. This includes your IP address, device model, OS version, app-install ID, and, where you allow it, your advertising identifier. Singular matches this to ad clicks or views to work out which campaign, if any, led to your install. We and Singular then send a limited set of in-game events, such as install, tutorial complete, and purchase, back to the ad platform that ran the campaign. The platform uses them to measure and optimize our campaigns.

We do not show third-party ads in the Games, and we do not sell your data for money. However, some US state laws treat sending identifiers and events to ad platforms for this purpose as “sharing” for “cross-context behavioral advertising” or “targeted advertising.” You can opt out of this (see below and Your US state privacy rights).

Your controls

  • iOS: We ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before reading your IDFA. If you decline, attribution on iOS uses Apple’s privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork / AdAttributionKit, and no advertising identifier is used. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: You can delete or reset your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some devices).
  • In the Games: Turn off Settings > Privacy > Share data for ad measurement. This stops us and Singular from sending your events to ad platforms, and applies on every device where you sign in.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: We ask for your consent before we read your advertising identifier or turn on attribution. If you don’t consent, no attribution data is collected about you. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy.

Singular’s privacy policy: singular.net/privacy-policy. Meta: facebook.com/privacy/policy. TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

  • US states: see Your US state privacy rights below.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: our processing relies on the legal bases described above. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Device controls: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier and manage tracking permissions in your device settings.

To exercise a right, email support@goodluckmachine.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

How to make a request. Email support@goodluckmachine.com from the email address linked to your account, or include your player ID (shown in Settings > Account). We use that to verify your identity. We may ask for one more detail that only the account holder would know, such as a recent purchase. If you play without an email-linked account, we can verify you through a code shown in the game. We don’t ask for ID documents.

Response times. We respond within one month in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and within 45 days in the US. We may extend these by the further period the law allows if a request is complex, and we’ll tell you if we do.

Complaints. If you’re in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

How to opt out of sharing and targeted advertising

  • Global Privacy Control: Our website doesn’t share personal information, so a GPC signal sent to it has nothing to switch off. To opt out of sharing in the Games, use the in-game setting above.

Other requests. Email support@goodluckmachine.com. We verify requests as described in Your choices and rights.

Authorized agents. You can use an authorized agent to make a request for you. We’ll ask for proof that you gave the agent permission, and we may ask you to confirm your identity directly with us.

Appeals. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with “Appeal” in the subject line. We’ll respond within the time your state’s law requires. If you’re unhappy with the result, you can contact your state attorney general.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, from within the Games (Settings) where available, or by emailing support@goodluckmachine.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject “Account deletion request.” Deletion is permanent and removes your progress and purchased in-game items, which cannot be restored.

Children

The Games are not directed to children. You must be at least 13 to play. In the UK, the minimum age is also 13. In the EEA, it’s the age set by your country for consenting to online services, which is between 13 and 16.

When you first launch a Game, we ask for your age without suggesting an answer. If you’re under the minimum age, you can’t create an account and we don’t collect personal data about you beyond what’s needed to block access. If you’re under 18, we turn off sharing for ad measurement and use privacy-protective defaults.

If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under the minimum age, we delete it. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email support@goodluckmachine.com.
