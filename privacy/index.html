Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data: an account identifier, display name, email address, and sign-in method (for example, an email login or a platform sign-in such as Apple or Google).
  • Support and communications: messages you send us and any details you include.

Data we collect automatically

  • Gameplay data: progress, in-game items and currency, settings, achievements, and how you interact with features.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, IP address (from which we infer approximate location such as country), time zone, and crash and performance information.
  • Identifiers: device and advertising identifiers provided by your operating system, and identifiers we generate for your install or account.
  • Purchase data: what you bought, when, and the transaction ID. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Website data: standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone or camera data.

How we use data

  • Provide the Games: create and secure your account, save progress, restore purchases, and deliver in-game items. (Legal basis under GDPR: performing our contract with you.)
  • Process purchases and prevent fraud or abuse. (Contract; legitimate interests.)
  • Analytics and improvement: understand how the Games are used, fix bugs, balance gameplay, and develop new features. (Legitimate interests, or consent where required.)
  • Support: respond to your questions and requests. (Contract; legitimate interests.)
  • Security and legal compliance: detect cheating and fraud, enforce our terms, and meet legal obligations. (Legitimate interests; legal obligation.)
  • Service communications: important notices about your account or the Games.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to show third-party advertising or to build advertising profiles.

Who we share data with

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, account authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and customer support tools. They may use the data only to provide services to us.
  • Platform providers such as Apple and Google, which operate the app stores, process payments, and handle sign-in if you choose it. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.
  • Legal and safety: authorities or other parties when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Games.
  • Business transfers: a successor, if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Other players may see your display name and in-game activity if the Games include social or multiplayer features.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and your data may be processed there and in other countries where we or our providers operate. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland.

Retention

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active and for a limited time afterward as needed to provide the Games, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we must keep it by law (for example, purchase records).

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

  • California and other US states: you may request to know what personal data we collected, used, and disclosed, and to delete or correct it. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: our processing relies on the legal bases described above. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Device controls: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier and manage tracking permissions in your device settings.

To exercise a right, email support@goodluckmachine.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, from within the Games (Settings) where available, or by emailing support@goodluckmachine.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject “Account deletion request.” Deletion is permanent and removes your progress and purchased in-game items, which cannot be restored.

Children

The Games are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK, or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy as our Games and legal requirements change. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify you in the Games or by email where appropriate.
