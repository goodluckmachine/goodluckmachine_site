Support

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Stuck, confused, or something broke in Two Minute Drill? This page is how to reach us and the quick answers people ask for most.

Contact us

Write to support@goodluckmachine.com. We reply within 3 business days.

To help us help you, include:

  • Your Player ID. In the game: Settings > Help & Privacy. Tap it to copy.
  • Your device and whether it’s an iPhone or an Android phone.
  • What happened, and what you expected instead. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in the same way you did before: with Game Center on iPhone, Google Play Games on Android, or your username and password. Playing as a guest ties your progress to that one device.

How do I change my name?

Open Settings in the game and edit your name there.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, or on the web if you can’t open it. Everything is on the Delete your account page.

What do you do with my data?

The Privacy Policy explains it in plain language.
